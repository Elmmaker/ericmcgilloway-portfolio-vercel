I · BRAND ARCHITECTURE

One crown. Every coast.

A master brand that travels — a destination skin that ignites.

The franchise lives in two layers. The master brand never changes: the crown, the rose, the idea that being chosen is being crowned. It is clean, timeless, and built to outlive any single location. The destination skin changes every season — same mark, same ceremony, dressed in the color and ornament of wherever love is unfolding.

The constant — every season

Crown of Roses

The mark · the rose-as-coronation premise · the ceremony · the type · the motion.

Owns the franchise logo and all cross-season marketing.

The variable — per destination

The local court

Localized wordmark · regional ornament · a spice palette drawn from the place.

Snaps onto the master. Never a teardown.

II · THE MASTER BRAND

The crown that travels light

Weightless romance. Ivory, cream, blush, champagne, a single thread of gold. Bridal without being literal, luxurious without being loud — a palette that flatters any destination instead of fighting it.

III · SEASON 01 — THE SKIN

Cabo San Lucas adds the spice

Same crown · localized in language and light

The master brand deepens into a desert-sunset world. The name slips into Spanish, the ornament becomes hand-painted Talavera and gold filigree, and the palette catches fire — maroon, rose-pink, marigold-orange. The romance stays clean and luminous; the place is what brings the heat.
